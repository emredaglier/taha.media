# taha.media

The portfolio of Taha, a video editor, motion designer and graphic designer: a public site in Turkish where he publishes his work, and a private admin where he manages it.

## Language

**Work** (İş):
One piece of Taha's work, published on the site with its own page.
_Avoid_: Project, case study, portfolio item, post

**Discipline** (Disiplin):
A craft a Work belongs to: Kurgu, Motion or Grafik. A Work has one or more.
_Avoid_: Category, type, tag

**Featured Work** (Öne çıkan iş):
A Work the Editor has chosen, and put in order, to appear on the home page. Home looks right with 6 to 12 of them; the Editor sees a warning outside that range, but saving is never blocked.
_Avoid_: Highlight, selected, pinned

**Showreel**:
Taha's reel of his best work: a short silent loop at the top of the home page, plus a link to the full-length version.
_Avoid_: Reel, hero video, intro video

**Gallery item** (Galeri öğesi):
A single image or short silent loop of Taha's visual work outside client projects, such as photography or a personal or experimental piece. It is not a Work: it has no page of its own, only an optional caption and year.
_Avoid_: Photo, post, gallery Work, shot

**Gallery** (Galeri):
The collection of all Gallery items, in the order the Editor sets.
_Avoid_: Archive, playground, lab, moodboard

**Editor**:
A person who manages content in the admin. Every Editor has the same rights.
_Avoid_: Admin, user, author

**Statement** (Tanıtım cümlesi):
The sentence on the home page with the Showreel clip inside it: the text before the clip and the text after it.
_Avoid_: Intro, tagline

### Presentation

**Preview loop** (Önizleme):
A short, silent clip of a Work that plays on its card while it is in view or hovered, and stands in for the cover image.
_Avoid_: Teaser, hover video, thumbnail video
